Evaluation Criteria

Five dimensions for selecting shopping tools

  • Warehouse inspection photo resolution and detail completeness across all intake angles to catch manufacturing flaws early before domestic return windows close permanently
  • Carrier line diversity and regional tax-free routing options to minimize customs overhead, ensure smooth delivery, and eliminate surprise destination tax assessments
  • Packaging customization options and volumetric reduction support to lower shipping costs significantly on bulky winter outerwear, shoe boxes, and structured accessories
  • Exchange and return processing turnaround times within active merchant policy windows to resolve sizing mistakes or damaged goods before storage deadlines expire
  • Catalog data freshness and community documentation depth across popular categories to ensure reliable seller discovery and accurate product option transcription
