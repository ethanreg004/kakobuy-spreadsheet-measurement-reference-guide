Quick FAQ

Essential questions before recording observations

Why is exact option transcription necessary?

Purchasing agents rely on exact merchant dropdown strings; paraphrasing options causes ordering discrepancies, wrong warehouse items, and lengthy return processing delays.

How frequently should saved rows be rechecked?

Volatile listings should be audited at regular intervals, while stable core items can follow standard periodic review cycles prior to submitting parcel consolidation orders.

What should happen when a saved link changes?

Append a new dated observation line preserving the historical record rather than overwriting previous data so price drift and option evolution remain fully transparent.

Operational Safeguards

Explicit pause conditions before saving records

Halt the documentation workflow whenever listing evidence fails to meet minimum observation thresholds to avoid contaminating your spreadsheet with undocumented assumptions.

  • The destination URL redirects to an unrelated product, search directory, or authentication wall that prevents direct verification.
  • Merchant dropdown options contain ambiguous labels that cannot be cross-referenced with the merchant product sizing chart.
  • Displayed prices omit mandatory domestic shipping or accessory surcharges from the baseline total checkout cost.
  • Product imagery consists entirely of computerized renders with zero physical item photographs showing genuine material quality.
